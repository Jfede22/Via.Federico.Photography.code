Accessibility

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Via Federico's Photography wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we do

  • Semantic page structure, a "Skip to main content" link and full keyboard navigation.
  • Descriptive alt text for images and labels for interactive controls.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators and a mobile menu that reports its open or closed state to screen readers.
  • Respect for the "reduce motion" setting: automatic slideshows do not auto-advance.
  • Responsive layout that works on phones, tablets and desktops, and with browser zoom.

Known limitations

Our contact form (Google Forms) and booking calendar (Setmore) are provided by third parties, and we cannot fully control their accessibility. Some muted text colors may have lower contrast than we would like, and we are working to improve them. If anything is hard to use, please tell us and we will offer another way to book or contact us, for example by email.

Feedback and help

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in another format, email Viafedericophotography@gmail.com with the page address and the problem you encountered. We try to respond within 2 business days and will work with you to resolve the issue.
