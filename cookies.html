Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies this website uses and how you can control them. Read it together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites. "Similar technologies" include local storage and tracking scripts. They can make a site work, remember your choices, or measure usage.

3. Your choices

  • Use Cookie Settings in the footer of any page to accept, reject or customize at any time. Turning a setting off stops future loading; to remove cookies already set by Google or Setmore, clear them in your browser.
  • Your browser's Global Privacy Control signal is honored: analytics remain off.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without optional cookies, but the contact form and booking calendar will not display; you can email Viafedericophotography@gmail.com or open the booking page on Setmore directly instead.
